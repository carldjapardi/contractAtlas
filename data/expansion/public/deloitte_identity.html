Enabling trusted identities in a connected and open world

Providing methodologies, services, and technologies along with services for implementation and operation of identity platforms to help your organization manage which employees, partners, suppliers, customers, consumers, and third parties can access sensitive organizational applications and data.

Benefits include:

  1. Secure, authorize, and streamline omnichannel access and interactions
  2. Maintain security with cloud migration, architecture, product development, and processes
  3. Increase visibility and control over customer data to foster digital trust and improve customer relationships
  4. Utilize data ethically, maintain privacy, and reduce biases related to deep-learning algorithms

With Cyber Identity, we focus on empowering your people to work together with greater insight, agility, and resilience to threats across your entire enterprise.

Secure, compliant, and scalable identity approach

We offer processes and technology supporting the authentication, authorization, and auditing of employees, contractors, and customers

Digital Identity +

Streamline the entire identity life cycle for regulatory compliance, unified security, and Zero Trust protection.

Customer Identity Access Management (CIAM)

Creates defensible position for GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, and other regulations, which can help improve customer engagement, retention, trust, and brand strength while providing predictable experiences that remove friction from the customer journey.

MORE DETAILS

Identity Strategy

Includes user life cycle management and provisioning and processes for onboarding, transfer, validation, offboarding, and self-service functions; supports digital and customer identity management.

Emerging Identity Trust Services

Supports emerging trends and incorporates into identity and access management (IAM) road map.

  • Identity for the Internet of Things (IOT)
  • Customer profile management and consent management
  • User behavioral analytics
  • Replatforming Identity-as-a-Service (IDaaS) hosted services

MORE DETAILS

Access and Advanced Authentication

Includes support for access controls, password management, authentication, authorization, single sign-on, and federation, as well as policy configuration and service monitoring.

Identity Governance and Administration

Includes support for access governance and certification campaigns, remediation action plans supported with role mining, development of audit-proof processes and continuous monitoring processes.

Privileged Account Management

Includes administration of “keys to the kingdom” accounts, support workflows for elevated access, temporary elevation of privileges, log management, and reporting and auditing of privileged access.

 Identity: Reducing risk by building trust

Blending of digital and physical worlds for new possibilities with a new vision of identity required to improve trust and deliver.                         
Success story: Enhanced Productivity, Security and Cost Efficiency

SailPoint was implemented to improve user productivity, security, and compliance by concentrating several IAM solutions. A unified UI serves 100k users globally across 1k+ services via centralization. Over 2k applications were migrated through an online intake platform that has facilitated improved onboarding speed and user satisfaction. Reduced technology usage from 21 to 5 with consolidated IAM, UI deployment and global control catalog.

Success story: Streamlining IAM and Ensuring Compliance

Advised client on divestitures, mergers, and outdated technologies. Leveraging Digital Identity+ tools on AWS GovCloud (FedRamp) we efficiently managed and operated the IAM environment using IdentityIQ and IdentityNow. IGA solution streamlined user identity lifecycle for 120,000+ employees and contractors across 40+ countries providing compliance with stringent aerospace industry regulations. Migrated legacy platforms and simplified access reviews, met the SOX, DFARs, and CMMC requirements, achieving cost savings through solution decommissioning.

Success story: Driving Cyber and IAM Transformation

Successfully migrated IAM from Sun IDM to SailPoint IdentityIQ as part of a broad cyber/IAM transformation. Streamlined risk assessment, redesigned cert operations, and achieved automation of user and IAM life cycle management processes. Provided assistance to enable self-service capabilities, integrated with ServiceNow and implemented periodic access recertifications.

The Deloitte difference

Assist organizations with identity and access programs and implement technologies in order to help provide appropriate access and prevent fraud and identity-related risks.

Outcomes-driven

Reduce operating costs: With IAM and insider threat detection, we help you reduce costs related to security operations, user onboarding, user self-service, offboarding, and fraud monitoring.
Decrease credential risk: Deloitte’s Privileged Access Management (PAM) program includes automated password management, post-breach response time, and forensic execution capabilities, which protect priority credentials and significantly reduce risk.

Quality-oriented

Consistent compliance: Identity Governance and Administration (IGA) helps you address privacy, security, and compliance requirements for highly complex environments.
Easy-to-use, secure options: Tapping into our relationship with leading vendors and our proprietary offerings (e.g., Digital Identity+) you can enjoy a variety of technology options that provide high levels of security and ease of use for diverse workforce environments.

Value-focused

Rapid solutioning with flexible delivery approaches: We can work with your organization to define your vision for trusted customer experiences and facilitate a rapid process.
Insights for improved customer engagement and brand: We lead the market in understanding the trust imperative and the interconnectivity between security, user experiences, and brand sentiment.
