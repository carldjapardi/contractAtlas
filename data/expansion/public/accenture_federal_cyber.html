Defend the mission at the speed of machine threats

The AI imperative

AI-driven threats move too fast for manual response. As adversaries deploy advanced machine-learning tools to identify and exploit software vulnerabilities in minutes, federal agencies must move past traditional security paradigms. Achieving true resilience requires automation and closed-loop security operations that protect critical assets at machine speed.

The new federal cyber imperatives

slideText 1 ofText / 1

Frontier AI & autonomous exploit defense

Frontier AI has shrunk attacker timelines from months to minutes, so signature-based defense isn’t enough. Agencies need agentic application security that continuously validates, hardens and defends code before flaws are disclosed or weaponized.

Securing critical infrastructure & xOT

As IT and OT converge, federal utility, energy and defense environments face a larger, more fragile attack surface. Protecting xOT requires deep firmware visibility and automated exposure assessments that improve security without causing mission downtime.

Post-quantum cryptographic readiness

“Harvest now, decrypt later” makes quantum risk a near-term issue for encrypted federal data. Agencies must inventory crypto assets and shift to adaptable workflows using NIST-approved, FIPS-aligned algorithms to protect data for the long term.

Adaptive security operations & MxDR

Human-only alert triage can’t keep up with automated, AI-driven attacks. An Adaptive Security posture with MxDR correlates telemetry, isolates endpoints and drives closed-loop remediation in seconds to contain threats faster.

Identity & ICAM as the zero trust control plane

With network boundaries fading, ICAM is the center of federal cybersecurity and Zero Trust. Moving to phishing-resistant MFA and attribute-based access control (ABAC) enables faster, audit-ready Zero Trust compliance for both people and AI agents.

Execution-ready cyber capabilities

We deploy operational capabilities that detect exposures before public disclosure, execute closed-loop remediation without human latency and maintain mission uptime against nation-state exploit chains. Our cleared U.S.-based teams deliver proof-of-defense through five execution-ready plays.

Translate federal mission into overall cyber architecture, providing the blueprint for change. We deliver mission-aligned security architecture, enterprise governance, cyber leadership, risk and regulatory strategy and FedRAMP advisory services.

Modernize and efficiently manage customer, workforce, and non-human identities, including AI agents. We secure federal mission, workforce and ecosystem identities through a trust exchange that provides advanced credential management and verification.

Enable trust, resilience and compliance across emerging technologies. Our services focus on securing AI and agents, leveraging AI for cybersecurity and protecting high-performance and quantum computing from advanced and future threats.

Protect the mission as it transforms by securing enterprise technology and processes. We provide cloud, application, network and data security, along with Zero Trust architecture, secure infrastructure and post-quantum cryptography.

Identify and remediate exposures to reduce risk, while driving adaptive cyber defense and enabling rapid response and recovery from threats. Our capabilities include cyber exposure testing, threat intelligence and modern cyber defense operations.

Protect high-consequence cyber missions, securing the tactical edge and ensuring operational reliability. We secure industrial control systems, connected physical products, physical AI, space assets, weapon systems and the secure edge.
