Frontier AI & autonomous exploit defense
Frontier AI has shrunk attacker timelines from months to minutes, so signature-based defense isn’t enough. Agencies need agentic application security that continuously validates, hardens and defends code before flaws are disclosed or weaponized.
Securing critical infrastructure & xOT
As IT and OT converge, federal utility, energy and defense environments face a larger, more fragile attack surface. Protecting xOT requires deep firmware visibility and automated exposure assessments that improve security without causing mission downtime.
Post-quantum cryptographic readiness
“Harvest now, decrypt later” makes quantum risk a near-term issue for encrypted federal data. Agencies must inventory crypto assets and shift to adaptable workflows using NIST-approved, FIPS-aligned algorithms to protect data for the long term.
Adaptive security operations & MxDR
Human-only alert triage can’t keep up with automated, AI-driven attacks. An Adaptive Security posture with MxDR correlates telemetry, isolates endpoints and drives closed-loop remediation in seconds to contain threats faster.
Identity & ICAM as the zero trust control plane
With network boundaries fading, ICAM is the center of federal cybersecurity and Zero Trust. Moving to phishing-resistant MFA and attribute-based access control (ABAC) enables faster, audit-ready Zero Trust compliance for both people and AI agents.
